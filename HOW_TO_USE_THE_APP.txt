ThermoCubic - HOW TO USE OUR APP :)
made by Vaatsalya and Jiya

So you downloaded our app. Great choice. Here's how to get it running in
under a minute. 🚀

STEP 1: Open the folder 📂
After unzipping, you will see a folder called RealGasStudio. Everything
lives inside it, so please don't pull app.py out on its own. It needs its
friends.

STEP 2: Point your terminal at the folder 💻
Open a terminal (the VS Code terminal works great) and go inside:

    cd RealGasStudio

This step matters: Python looks for app.py inside the folder, so if you
run it from anywhere else it won't find the file.

STEP 3: Run it ▶️

    python app.py

That's it. Your browser opens ThermoCubic at http://127.0.0.1:8000 🎉

What you need:
- Python 3 (version 3.6 or newer). That's all. No pip install, no extra
  packages. Yes, really. 🙌

How to stop:
Press Ctrl+C in the terminal and the app goes to sleep. 😴

What the app does:
- Pick a gas (we have 11), pick an equation of state (van der Waals,
  Redlich-Kwong, SRK or Peng-Robinson), enter T and P.
- You get molar volume, compressibility factor Z, enthalpy H, entropy S,
  fugacity f and the fugacity coefficient phi.
- Compare all 4 equations side by side, plot 6 different graphs, and check
  your calculation history. The app remembers you. 🧠

Bonus stuff:
- Run the tests with `python test_eos.py`. They check our calculations
  against the textbook examples. Spoiler: they pass. ✅
- Your history is saved in history.db, right next to app.py.
- All equations and constants come from Smith, Van Ness, Abbott and
  Swihart, 8th edition (Table 3.1, Eq. 13.74, Tables B.1 and C.1). 📖
- H and S are relative to the ideal gas at 298.15 K and 1 bar, where
  H = 0 and S = 0.
- The logo is the official IIT Delhi emblem, from
  https://home.iitd.ac.in/images/preloaders/logo.png

Now go predict some real gases. Have fun! 🧪🔥
